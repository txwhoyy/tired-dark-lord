Morwen, toll-witch of the Ashen Crossing, has collected debts for three hundred years — tonight a hero comes to pay the ninth toll, and she means to collect it properly. Every threat is still real: the curses, the red ink, the little skull beside your name. She simply cannot remember where she put the ledger.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
